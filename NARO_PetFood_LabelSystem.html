Label System
NARO's label system is built on a single principle: the protein drives everything. Each SKU has its own dominant color identity, hero ingredient illustration zone, and benefit claim — but shares the same structural grid, typeface family, and information hierarchy. A shopper should be able to identify NARO from 3 metres and distinguish the variant from 1 metre.
SKU 01
🍗
Chicken
Free-Range Chicken
Everyday Premium
High-protein formula for active adult dogs. 42% real chicken, no artificial preservatives. Lead SKU for retail entry. Warm amber palette signals energy and approachability.
SKU 02
🐟
Salmon
Wild Atlantic Salmon
Omega-Rich · Coat Health
Omega-3 & 6 enriched formula for coat health and cognitive support. 38% wild salmon. Deep teal palette signals freshness, ocean provenance, and functional premium.
SKU 03
🥩
Lamb
Grass-Fed Lamb
Sensitive Digestion
Single-protein formula for dogs with sensitive digestion. 40% grass-fed lamb, no grains. Deep burgundy palette signals care, gentleness, and clinical authority.
