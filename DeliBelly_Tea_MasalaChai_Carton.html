The Brief
Deli Belly knows
what North India
tastes like.
Deli Belly has spent years feeding Pune's appetite for authentic North Indian food — the kind of food that comes with a mandatory chai break. The launch of Deli Belly Tea is not a category stretch. It is the natural next chapter: bringing the same philosophy of honest, spiced, uncompromising flavour to the morning cup.
The Masala Chai variant is the anchor SKU. It is built on a 7-spice blend that mirrors the dhaba-style chai that Deli Belly's kitchen team has been brewing for staff since day one. That recipe — turmeric, ginger, cardamom, cinnamon, clove, black pepper, and star anise — is now inside every tea bag.
The packaging brief: premium enough for a gift, honest enough for a daily shelf. The reverse tuck-end carton format was chosen for structural security and clean front-panel impact at retail. The color language borrows from the spice rack — saffron, burgundy, turmeric gold — grounded in cream to signal purity of ingredients.
